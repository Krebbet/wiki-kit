# fal (Features & Labels Inc.) — Generative-Media Inference, $1.3B Series F at $16B (2026-11-03)

fal is a generative-media inference platform (image, video, audio models behind one API). On 2026-11-03 it announced a Series F of $1.3B at a $16B valuation, led by Bessemer Venture Partners, with Nvidia participating as a "strategic" investor. Valuation is reported as doubled since the April 2026 Series E ($350M at $8B). Annualized revenue ">$800M" is a single anonymous-source figure, not company-confirmed. Watch: it counters the 2026-04 framing that the 2024 inference cohort is "won or commoditised" (see [[open-questions-2026-04]] C11).

## Business claims

- **Product (2026-11-03, company description):** cloud platform for running image, video, audio generation models via a single API. Customers named by category only: entertainment studios, ad agencies, game developers.
- **Post-training:** in-house researchers post-train some hosted models for speed; focus over the past year is video, in partnership with open- and closed-source model developers. Some deals pay fal licensing fees or revenue share (Bloomberg-reported).
- **Revenue:** annualized revenue passed $800M "earlier this year" per an unnamed person familiar, via Bloomberg. Unconfirmed by the company; no date given.
- **Capacity:** CEO Burkay Gur (Bloomberg interview): "We hit a point in summer where we were not able to serve our customers and had to rate-limit them." Round sized for compute and hiring beyond 185 employees.
- **Own pretraining:** not near-term; not ruled out (Gur).
- **Margins:** several unnamed people told TechCrunch sector gross margins stay thin because compute is expensive to buy or lease. No fal-specific margin disclosed.
- **Peer marker:** valuation sits just behind Fireworks AI ($1.5B at $17.5B, July 2026).
- **Founders:** Gur (CEO, ex-Coinbase ML lead, ex-Oracle) and Gorkem Yurtseven (CTO, ex-Amazon), founded 2021.

## Read

Demand-constrained-by-compute story with a thin-margin sector backdrop; the $800M ARR figure does the heavy lifting for the valuation (~20x annualized) and rests on one anonymous source. Staleness: all figures dated 2026-11-03.
