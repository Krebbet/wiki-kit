# LangChain

Agent-engineering company (open-source LangChain framework from 2022, LangGraph orchestration, LangSmith platform for running, testing, observing and governing agents). Announced a $300M Series C at a $3B valuation led by CapitalG (Alphabet's growth fund), reported 2026-11-13/14, bringing total funding to ~$460M. Valuation is more than 2x the $1.25B set about a year earlier ($125M, IVP-led). All figures are company-announced or press-relayed; none independently verified. The round prices agent observability and orchestration as a standalone venture category even as hyperscalers ship the same features natively.

**Tier:** startup.

## Round facts (reported 2026-11-13, TNW citing the company blog, TechCrunch and WSJ)

- Size / valuation: $300M Series C at $3B; total raised ~$460M.
- Lead: CapitalG (existing investor).
- Also in: GIC, DFJ Growth, Salesforce Ventures, Insight Venture Partners, QuantumLight, MongoDB Ventures (new); IVP and Sapphire Ventures (existing); Sequoia, Amplify and Benchmark added more.
- Step-up: from $1.25B (~late 2025, $125M round led by IVP) to $3B, about 2.4x in roughly a year *(editorial arithmetic)*.
- Use of proceeds: LangSmith, plus hiring. Headcount 435 full-time, target 850 by end-2027 (WSJ via TNW); 47 forward deployed engineers and 13 deployed architects.
- Product event: new LangSmith features promised at a release on 2026-11-20.

## Product footprint

- LangChain (open-source framework, 2022), LangGraph (agent orchestration), LangSmith (run/test/observe/govern; framework- and model-agnostic).
- Claim: 7,000+ organisations use LangSmith (company, 2026-11). Named: Harvey, Rippling, Listen Labs, Toyota, Nvidia, Salesforce, Workday. Workday's cut in time-to-deploy a new agent from five months to under a day is a company statement. "Use" is undefined: no paid-seat, revenue or ARR figure is disclosed.
- Existing wiki context: listed under "Observability / tooling" in [[ai-app-categories-2025]]; LangGraph is a named supported framework and LangSmith an OTel export target in [[aws-bedrock-agentcore-june-2026]]; LangChain appears among vulnerable agent frameworks in [[mcp-rce-supply-chain-2026-05]]; a DXC partnership (2026-10-14) was noted only as minor in the 2026-10-21 weekly brief.

## Category readthrough

- A $3B mark for a framework-agnostic observability/orchestration layer is a bet against commoditisation by cloud-native tooling (AgentCore Observability and Foundry already bundle tracing and evals). Revenue is undisclosed, so the multiple cannot be computed.
- Relation to open question C11 (BVP "first-wave / next-wave" inflation, in [[open-questions-2026-04]]): C11 concerns the inference-infra cohort, not agent tooling, so this does not resolve it. It is a data point that late-stage money still pays up for a layer others call commodity, which is consistent with C11's worry that valuation narratives outrun disclosed revenue. No update to C11's claim either way.

## Skepticism flags

- Investor incentives: CapitalG is Alphabet's fund, and Alphabet competes in agents (see [[google-a2a-agentic-foundation-2026-08]]); Salesforce Ventures and MongoDB Ventures are strategic investors whose parents integrate with or resell this layer. Existing investors (IVP, Sapphire, Sequoia, Benchmark, Amplify) leading or topping up an up-round mark their own books *(editorial)*.
- Quotes ("insanely competitive market", agent behaviour "is the hard part to control") are from the CEO and a CapitalG partner, not neutral parties.
- Source is a secondary rewrite; the company blog and TechCrunch were not read directly.
