# Cognition AI (Devin)

Cognition AI, maker of the autonomous coding agent **Devin**, closed a **$2B Series E at a $48B valuation** (2026-09-08) — just four months after a $1B round at $26B (2026-05-27). Led by a16z and Accel, with Founders Fund, General Catalyst, and Avenir participating. Founded 2024 by Scott Wu.

## What it is

Devin is sold to enterprises as an autonomous software-engineering agent. This funding round is a market-structure story, not a product-capability one — the source (TechCrunch, 2026-09-08) does not detail specific product features. Named enterprise customers: Mercedes-Benz, NASA, Goldman Sachs, Citi.

Cognition is training its own model based on open-source alternatives — the same playbook [[cursor]] ran before its SpaceX acquisition — aimed at reducing reliance on third-party models from OpenAI and Anthropic. It leases an Nvidia server cluster costing "hundreds of millions of dollars annually," which The Information estimates could push total 2026 cash burn to ~$800M; training its own model is framed as a future path to breakeven.

## Market reception

- Annualized run-rate revenue reportedly grew from **$492M (May 2026) to $900M (September 2026)** — Cognition's own disclosure; TechCrunch notes the company didn't explain its run-rate calculation methodology.
- The Information projects Cognition reaching **$4B–$5B** annualized revenue by end of 2026, versus [[cursor]]'s reported trajectory toward **>$6B**.
- $48B valuation / $900M ARR is a higher revenue multiple than Cursor's spring-2026 $50B-valuation-talk / $2B ARR position.
- **Investor signal:** a16z — a major Cursor backer that profited from the SpaceX acquisition (see [[../landscape/spacex-cursor-60b-option-2026-04|spacex-cursor-60b-option-2026-04]]) — is now leading a round in Cursor's direct competitor. TechCrunch frames this as evidence VCs see AI coding as a multi-winner market, not winner-take-all.

## Hype-vs-reality delta

TechCrunch explicitly questions the ARR figure's rigor (no disclosed calculation method) and flags unresolved compute-constraint risk: unclear whether Cognition will hit the same GPU-access wall that forced Cursor to sell to SpaceX. Cash burn (~$800M/yr estimated) is high relative to disclosed revenue.

## Build-vs-buy signals

Not directly addressed in available reporting — this is a funding story, not an integration or pricing review. Watch for a product-capability source before advising on Devin adoption specifically.
