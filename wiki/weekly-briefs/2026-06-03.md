- **Hyperscaler AI capex entered a new magnitude regime.** Alphabet raised $85B in a single equity offering — oversubscribed by 13% in the first tranche ($45B vs $40B planned), with Berkshire Hathaway buying $10B. $180–190B in capex is Alphabet's 2026 plan. Simultaneously, Anthropic's Series H closed at $65B/$965B — the pre-announcement estimates ($30B/$900B) were off by a factor of two. Three AI IPOs (Anthropic S-1 filed June 1, OpenAI in queue, SpaceX) are testing whether institutional markets can absorb $3T+ in AI equity simultaneously.
- **Every major enterprise platform is racing to own the "employee agent" slot.** Snowflake (CoWork), Microsoft (Scout), Google (Gemini Spark), Salesforce (Agentforce) are all shipping "always-on personal agent for every knowledge worker" narratives within the same month. The differentiation axes: data substrate (Snowflake's governed data cloud vs Microsoft Graph vs Google Workspace), agent governance model (Snowflake Natoma MCP gateway, Microsoft policy conformance system, Google enterprise DLP), and lock-in mechanism (Snowflake's learned user context vs Microsoft's OpenClaw-based identity persistence).
- **MCP-as-infrastructure is becoming a structural enterprise bet.** Snowflake announced intent to acquire Natoma — an enterprise MCP gateway providing identity-aware authorization and auditability for every AI agent tool call. This is the fifth named enterprise incumbent choosing MCP-first (SAP, ServiceNow, Lyrie, Microsoft Copilot Studio, now Snowflake Natoma). The governance-layer-atop-MCP pattern that C17 has been watching is now being acquired, not just built.
- **OpenAI's enterprise knowledge-worker pivot is showing scale.** 5M weekly Codex users (6x since February), with knowledge workers at 20% of users and growing 3x faster than developers. Six role-specific plugins (finance, sales, design, analytics, etc.) + Sites (hosted interactive output) + Codex GA on Bedrock at parity pricing — three weeks after the $4B+ OpenAI Deployment Company launch. The API → platform → services stack is now operational.
- **The flat-subscription model for AI developer tools is breaking down.** Anthropic is ending flat-rate Claude Code access June 15 (replaced by credit pools). Microsoft reportedly canceled Claude Code licenses across its enterprise. Both signal enterprise AI tooling is entering the renegotiation phase — the pricing model that launched the coding-agent wave is no longer sustainable at agentic compute consumption rates.
