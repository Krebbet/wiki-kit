- **Critics are back.** S3AC (2610.35518, captured): token-level critic + difficulty-aware prompt sampling beats GRPO variants from 1.5B to 397B and sustains single-rollout training at 35B. Second data point for [[conflicts/sp3o-critic-repair-vs-critic-free-trend]].
