# Trends in the industry

- **A measured analog-PCM SoC appeared — the first PCM silicon in this wiki.** MEGATRON (Bologna/Chips-IT/STMicroelectronics, 28 nm FD-SOI, 4Mi-cell PCM macro + RISC-V) claims 57.5 TOPS/W, but the abstract states no boundary for that figure and the large-model results are from a simulated configuration; it bears on [[../devices/memristor-array-integration-gap]].
- **Digital neuromorphic hardware produced on-chip results from academic labs, with the usual power-accounting gaps.** Loihi 2 on-chip Q-learning (">1000x" dynamic-only; ~67x recomputed at board level) and a SpiNNaker event-camera pinball player (148 µW is an assumed per-event estimate) both extend [[../conflicts/snn-energy-payoff]].
- **Spintronics entered the wiki.** A V-MTJ + 22 nm CMOS Izhikevich neuron reports 145 fJ/spike from measured device data plus circuit simulation; the 88.6% spike-reduction claim is a software-proxy result. *(synthesis)* MTJ/MRAM now needs its own device page if a second source arrives.
- **Local-rule, backprop-free SNN training keeps getting pushed deeper.** A TTFS convolutional SNN trained with STDP/R-STDP only, with code released but no hardware or energy numbers.
- **Synthesis-only accelerators continue to appear without silicon** (e.g. MorphAtt, 32 nm, 20–29 TOPS/W claimed). *(synthesis)*
- **Industry was quiet.** BrainChip AKD1500 ecosystem demos with no numbers; no new Intel, SynSense, Innatera or Prophesee items; Lava-successor SDK still unannounced; Open Neuromorphic published nothing technical.
