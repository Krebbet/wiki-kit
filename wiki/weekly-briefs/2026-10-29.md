# Trends in the industry

Quiet week for industry news; the signal is in device/chip papers. Nothing below is padded.

- **Evidenced: BEOL integration of ferroelectric hafnia is being pushed onto unmodified commercial CMOS dies, at single-device level.** ETH Zurich (arXiv:2610.37530, 2026-10-27) post-processes two-terminal W/TiN/WOx/HZO-multilayer/Mo synapses onto XFAB XP018 180 nm dies; NMOS stay functional after a 500 °C anneal (saturation current −6% and Vth up to +60 mV on short-channel devices; reference-die spread not measured). Programming <1.8 V, 2 h retention, 1000-cycle endurance only; MNIST accuracy is software simulation. Answers viability-timeline: foundry gate looks tractable on a legacy node, no array or 1T-1R yet. 180 nm AlCu BEOL tolerates more heat than scaled nodes, so it does not transfer. *(device level; boundary: single device, 2000–5000 µm²)*
- **Evidenced: small measured neuromorphic-chip demos keep arriving with narrowly scoped energy numbers.** TEXEL (Groningen, arXiv:2610.36999): 482 nJ/inference is dynamic energy of one core's read-out layer only; 27 µW is chip static, so at 1 Hz sampling static power dominates (synthesis: ~1.8%). 74.6% on-chip accuracy has no on-chip baseline (majority class ≈ 66.7%, synthesis). The "matches RBF SVM" claim holds only for a 64-bit read-out, not the 1-bit one on chip. Loihi 1 birdsong matching (arXiv:2610.38564): 8.71 J / 0.75 W is the Loihi network only, no CPU baseline. Both answer research-relevance; neither moves a viability date.
- **Evidenced: FPGA SNN energy wins come from arithmetic/area, not spike sparsity.** The mmWave ISAC accelerator (arXiv:2610.37480) shows 3.4×/7.6× lower PL dynamic energy than same-device ANNs, over idle only, with static power dominant and accuracy ~1.5 points optimistic. FPGA, not neuromorphic silicon; feeds [[conflicts/snn-energy-payoff]] as a boundary datapoint, not a resolution.

No Intel Lava-successor SDK or next-gen Loihi announcement seen. Coverage caveat: the industry lane did not fetch ASX or trade-press pages directly, so absence is weak evidence.
