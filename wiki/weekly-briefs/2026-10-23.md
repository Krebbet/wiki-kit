# Trends in the industry

- **On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) now has a theory-and-diagnostics layer.** Minimax bounds for reverse-KL distillation (2610.30844) and offline KL metrics that predict which privileged information helps (2610.22512); MCPD (2610.30880, watchlisted) targets reference-sensitive teacher targets. Touches [[research/teacher-student-rl/privileged-info-opsd]] and the OPSA-vs-OPD conflict: gains depend on real teacher content, not only logp suppression.
- **OPD + RL is being unified at the gradient level.** GIFT (2610.30796) filters distillation tokens by conflict with the RL gradient and anneals to pure RL; TreeOPSD (2610.23181) recovers the policy-gradient direction via a bounded teacher. Bears on [[research/teacher-student-rl/sequential-opd-then-rl]] (sequential vs joint).
- **Teacher-agnostic privileged distillation.** BSD (2610.26397) works with cross-tokenizer and closed-API teachers via verifier-gated reweighting of failed rollouts — relevant to the single-sample thesis where the "teacher" is one trace.
- **Expert-prefix hints are becoming the standard way to mix off-policy data into GRPO** (MOER 2610.30873, watchlisted), a hint-curriculum cousin of [[research/curriculum-and-decomposition/_overview]] *(synthesis)*.
- **Attribution studies challenge "RL created X".** "Wait" is inherited from mid-training/SFT, not invented by RL (2610.30882); counterpart to Invisible-Leash / Yue on RL-as-selection, and a caution for marker-based concept-acquisition evidence.
