- **The OPD generalization/correction debate deepened via two independent mechanisms this week.** OPDVR (ReLU-gated OPD+RLVR hybrid) formally proves and empirically shows a student exceeding its teacher on AIME24; "Every Coin Has Two Sides" adds an origin-compatibility axis (same- vs. cross-origin teacher/student transfer) and a multi-teacher routing "seesaw" failure mode. - **Self-play sees two independent curriculum/environment-generation entries.** LURE (pursuit-evasion framing, RL-trained evader positions tasks at the solver's capture frontier) and SPADE (LLM plays designer + agent, GRPO-trained on self-generated environments) both attack the same problem — automatic difficulty curricula without human-curated tasks — from different angles. LURE adds the wiki's twelfth proposer-reward shape and also disputes the wiki's own characterization of R-Zero's challenger mechanism, opening [[../conflicts/lure-vs-r-zero-challenger-mechanism]].
- **A method-class challenge to the Invisible Leash bound, not a domain-specific one.** Evolution Strategies — trained on the identical verifier-reward objective as GRPO, but never sampling trajectories from $\pi_\theta$ itself — exceeds base-model Pass@16/32 in several settings. Because Theorem C.1's proof is stated for trajectory-sampling gradient methods (REINFORCE/PPO/GRPO/DAPO/REINFORCE++), this reads as an open scope question rather than a resolved counter-example: is the "invisible leash" a property of *verifier-reward RLVR broadly*, or specifically of *on-policy gradient sampling*? Extends [[../conflicts/invisible-leash-vs-spiral-transfer]] with a genuinely new escape-channel candidate.
- **Reward/data-quality robustness looks far more model- and task-dependent than either side of the literature currently claims.** ReViSQL-K2.6's industrial-scale finding (32.8% false-positive execution-match rewards measurably hurt RLVR training) sits opposite Spurious Rewards' Qwen2.5-Math result (fully random rewards still recover 73% of ground-truth gains). Neither paper's protocol has been tested on the other's setting — opens [[../conflicts/revisql-vs-spurious-rewards-noise-robustness]] as a genuinely open empirical question, not a contradiction to explain away.

# Top 3 from the watchlist

1. **LURE (Pursuit-Evasion Self-Play for Zero-Data LLM Reasoning)** — reframes self-play as pursuit-evasion with a learned capture-frontier curriculum and dense verifier-progress credit; beats R-Zero/GRPO at 80% of R-Zero's rollout budget, and its own eval-integrity audit finds R-Zero's ZebraLogic gains were largely gold-answer leakage (68.0%→6.0%). *(new, arXiv:2608.21871)*
2. **OPDVR (On-policy Distillation with Verifiable Reward)** — a hyperparameter-free ReLU gate repairs sampled-token OPD's mis-signed implicit reward into a genuine RLVR method; formally proven never anti-aligned with the verifier gradient, and empirically exceeds the teacher on AIME24. *(new, arXiv:2608.24696)*
3. **Understanding Evolution Strategies for LLM Reasoning** — first ES entry in the wiki; ES exceeds base-model Pass@16/32 via population diversity rather than trajectory sampling, disputing Abdi et al. 2026's ES-causes-forgetting claim and posing a method-class scope question for the Invisible Leash bound. *(new, arXiv:2608.27351)*
