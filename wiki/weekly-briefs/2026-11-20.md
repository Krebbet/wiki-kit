# Trends in the industry

- **A count predicts when sparse-reward RL takes off.** 2611.26084 (captured): in a controlled Bayesian-player world, a run takes off iff enough sampled groups contain a success (~35 groups for even odds); repeating problems helps, restarts hurt. Gives a quantitative handle on 1-shot RLVR ([[research/single-sample-rl-finetuning/1-shot-rlvr]]) and a regime-dependent reading of [[conflicts/invisible-leash-vs-spiral-transfer]] (tiny synthetic world, so scope-limited).
- **OPD's cross-domain failure is behavioural, not just optimizer-level.** 2611.19244 (captured): ALFWorld-OPD collapses τ² (41.5→6.6) by over-generalising ReAct habits the teacher doesn't show; selecting tokens by the teacher's RL log-ratio Δ partly recovers it. Bears on [[conflicts/opsa-vs-opd-pattern-transfer]] and warns about single-source specialisation.
- **Multi-teacher OPD is being made cheaper.** 2611.24059 MRPD (captured): ordered replay of expert logs + dense reverse-KL matches MOPD at ~⅓ the cost; log order matters more than amount. M3OPD (2611.23034, watchlisted) is the open recipe. Expert RL logs as an implicit curriculum touches [[research/synthesis/concept-curriculum-method]].
- **Reward errors transfer by structure, not rate.** 2611.25191 (captured): per-answer judge errors stay local, but category-coherent false rejections generalise from 4–12 verdicts; a judge-free own-vote reward matches an answer key. Candidate resolution axis for [[conflicts/revisql-vs-spurious-rewards-noise-robustness]] *(synthesis)*.
- **Forgetting work moves to geometry and replay selection.** CurvDelta/CurvLoRA 2611.24654 (captured): update confined to a K-FAC low-curvature subspace with a provable preservation bound — another R_w primitive for the selective-finetuning stack; CoLiR (2611.25989) selects replay prompts by policy improvement. Adjacent to [[conflicts/on-policy-forgetting-vs-lr-confound]].
- Few direct single-sample RL papers this week; the nearest are the take-off law and "general progress substitutes for specific data" (2611.24844, watchlisted).
