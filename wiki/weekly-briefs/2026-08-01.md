- **Open-weight frontier race intensifies, but not uniformly.** Moonshot's Kimi K3 (2.78T params, 104B active) is the largest open-weight release to date, prompting grassroots distributed-inference engineering (80×RTX 5090 over 25GbE). *(synthesis)* The same week, Qwen3.7-Flash broke from Alibaba's prior fully-open pattern — API-only, no weights or technical report — a notable strategic reversal running against the open-weights momentum everyone else is riding.
- **Distillation-critique line keeps maturing.** [[flux-opd]] (context-evolving on-policy distillation via reverse-KL decomposition) and "Distilled RL for LLM Post-training" (cross-family transfer limits) both probe where on-policy self-distillation breaks down, extending last week's "Denser ≠ Better" into a genuine skeptical subfield with several independent, qualitatively-different failure-mode diagnoses.
- **Self-improving-agent research turns empirical and more skeptical.** [[ai-agents-open-ended-research]]'s "shadow evaluations" give the first controlled failure-mode taxonomy for frontier agents attempting real unpublished research (author-graded 2/6 and 1/6 — unambiguous rejects, despite flawless engineering execution), while [[skill-self-play]] pushes the evolutionary co-training side of the same cluster forward. Together they sharpen the boundary between "verifiable engineering" agentic claims (where the wiki has strong positive results) and "open-ended research judgment" claims (where this week's evidence says agents aren't there yet).
- **Extreme-efficiency reproducibility stunts surface on r/MLScaling.** A full ternary-LM pretraining run on a 2017 consumer GPU (no FP32 master weights, no Adam moments) and a new open ternary training/serving engine (Tritium) are grassroots validation of BitNet-style efficiency claims happening outside frontier labs — below this week's capture bar, but worth watching if the line keeps producing reproducible artifacts.
