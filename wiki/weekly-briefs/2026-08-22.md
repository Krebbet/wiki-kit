- **Self-play and adaptive-environment design for agentic RL surged this week.** SPADE, EnvHarness, and OpenART all trended on alphaXiv within days of each other, each proposing automated environment generation/evolution rather than hand-authored RL tasks — a shift from "design the reward" to "design the world the agent trains in." *(synthesis)*
- **Reward-model design is shifting from absolute scoring to relative ranking.** [[rrc-reward-ranking]] reframes RL reward-signal construction around preference rankings rather than preference-token scalarization, closing a known gap between generative-RM judgment quality and RL performance — seeds a new "reward model design for RL" thread in this wiki, distinct from the existing token-credit-assignment cluster.
- **Formal theory is catching up to empirical test-time-scaling practice.** [[test-time-scaling-taxonomy]] unifies single-trajectory/leaf-level/prefix-level regimes practitioners have used ad hoc for two years, and in doing so forced a terminology cleanup: this wiki now explicitly distinguishes fixed-model "test-time scaling" from weight-updating "test-time training" ([[conflicts/test-time-scaling-vs-test-time-training-terminology]]).
- **"Frozen base + swappable specialist LoRA adapters" is moving from a fine-tuning trick to a production architecture.** [[macaron-v1]] pairs a 744B frozen base with self-routing LoRA specialists and couples harness (tool/prompt) search to adapter training via its MindForge RSI loop — joining a fast-growing "harness-as-training-target" cluster alongside [[skillopt]], [[memoharness]], [[argus-agentic-runtime]]. *(synthesis)*
