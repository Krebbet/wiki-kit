# Trends in the industry
- **Agent governance is becoming a vendor-owned control plane.** Snowflake (Cortex AI Gateway, Business Ontology, 11-03), Anthropic (Efficient mode and four admin cost controls on unified chat+Cowork, 11-11) and Google (cross-cloud Iceberg federation with Unity/Horizon/Glue, 11-04) all ship the governance and cost layer as the product, not an add-on. Much of it is still private or public preview.
- **Data-platform wars are shifting to catalog and ontology interop.** Google's zero-egress cross-cloud cache plus Iceberg federation targets Databricks and Snowflake lock-in; Snowflake answers with a semantic Business Ontology inside Horizon. (synthesis)
- **Inference capital keeps concentrating.** fal's $1.3B Series F at $16B (Bessemer, 11-03; valuation doubled since April) cuts against the wiki's "2024 inference cohort is commoditised" claim (C11).
- **Agent-security risk is moving from prompt injection to supply chain and selection.** Glow Labs' MCPhantom (11-03): coding agents install archived, unpatched MCP servers from training-data memory. Single secondary source, so treat the numbers as unverified.
- **Agent M&A is buying "last mile" capabilities.** Sierra/Anon (agents behind logins), Meta/Conductor (parallel coding agents), Five9/Level AI (~$325M), and reported Palo Alto/Clutch (agent credentials, ~$500M). All single-aggregator, unconfirmed. (synthesis)
