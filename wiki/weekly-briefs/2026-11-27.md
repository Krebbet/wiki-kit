# Trends in the industry

- **RL rollouts are becoming reusable training data.** 2611.33102 (captured): plain SFT on unfiltered rollouts, in order, tracks the online RLVR curve; offline policy-gradient replay drifts. Related reuse work appeared in 2611.26803 and 2611.34756. Suggests an acquired capability can be stored and sequenced as data, an implicit-curriculum signal for [[research/synthesis/concept-curriculum-method]] *(synthesis)*.
- **Several method gaps look like tuning or initialisation artefacts.** Retention-tuned SFT removes most reported forgetting (2611.34579, captured); saturated GRPO erases corrupted-SFT damage (2611.26756, captured, one 1B model). Both bear on open conflicts and argue for tuned baselines before crediting any new primitive in [[research/synthesis/proposed-method]] *(synthesis)*.
- **On-policy distillation is being re-grounded in outcomes and diagnosed.** VOPD (2611.30325, captured) scales teacher signal by verifier correlation for a ~1 pt gain; 2611.27125 analyses OPD failure dynamics; 2611.34777 targets all-fail groups. Uniform-outcome groups remain the weak spot, which is the usual single-sample case *(synthesis)*.
- **Few examples can be written into weights without gradients.** 2611.26841 (captured) has agents install the ICL task vector from 16 examples, near few-shot fine-tuning on finished runs but brittle to wording. A reference point for single-example imprinting and a wording-invariance test for any candidate method.
