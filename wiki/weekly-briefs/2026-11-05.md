That is 5.8× total / 13.5× dynamic, but idle is 2.30 mW vs 0.05 mW and the baseline is 2.7 points more accurate. Touches: research, viability. See [[chips/hyvox-xylo-speaker-id]].

Touches: research, viability. See [[devices/sot-mtj-multiweight-wafer-scale-synapse]].
