# Top 3 from the watchlist

1. **BrainScaleS-2 chiplet interconnect** — phit-level mixed spike/config traffic lets Heidelberg's analog neuromorphic system scale past a single die; all-simulation, worst-case MTBF drops to minutes at scale. *(new, arXiv:2609.13563)*
2. **ETHEREAL event-GNN processor** — first event-driven graph-neural-network accelerator chip; 25.6 µs/1.7 µJ per event, silicon-measured, but the "comparable accuracy" headline is simulated and trails an FP64 A100 baseline on every dataset shown. *(new, arXiv:2609.15241)*
3. **Hippocampus-inspired memristor SNN** — first neuronal-diversity, biologically-realistic resting-state SNN on real memristor hardware (179 neurons, 3 cell types, 18,316 physical devices); not task-trained, tuned via Optuna to match dynamics. *(new, arXiv:2609.16429)*
