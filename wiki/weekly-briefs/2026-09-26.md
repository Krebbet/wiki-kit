# Trends in the industry

- **RL post-training is being decoupled from the RL run itself.** [[poem-predicting-rl-outcomes]] predicts what RL on a *new* reward would produce by composing existing single-reward policies' log-ratios — no rollout, no retraining — while the watchlist's F²DR pushes full-pipeline reward shaping over sparse outcome reward. Both push cost out of the training loop rather than making the loop itself cheaper. *(synthesis)*
- **LLM-judge evaluation economics is maturing into its own subfield.** [[jev-as-a-judge]]'s confidence-gated cascade retains ~99% of a top judge's accuracy at under half its fee, joining the wiki's existing reward-model-design thread ([[rrc-reward-ranking]], [[debate-training-reward-hacking]]) in treating "how much can you trust a judge, and how cheaply" as a first-class research question rather than an evaluation afterthought.
- **Attention-internals rethinking continues past the KV-cache axis.** [[memory-attention]] removes the value projection entirely in favor of static token-indexed lookup memory — joining Engram/DeepEmbed/PLE in the "capacity via lookup, not dense compute" line, and explicitly distinct from this wiki's recurrent/compressive long-context memory cluster despite superficially similar NIAH gains.
- **Data-free pretraining lands a genuinely adaptive-curriculum result.** [[self-play-pretraining-zero-data]] shows predictable power-law scaling of zero-shot loss on *natural* data purely from self-play over UTM programs with zero natural data ever seen — a stronger result than prior fixed-prior universal pretraining, and complementary to [[reinforcement-pretraining]]'s RL-at-pretraining line rather than competing with it.
- **Evaluation rigor is becoming its own research target across the evolutionary/agentic space.** [[evolutionary-search-eval-rigor]] formalizes why single-seed benchmarking misleads LLM-evolutionary-search claims and surfaces a validator exploit invisible below a seed-count threshold — a Henderson-et-al.-2018-style reckoning arriving for the AlphaEvolve/FunSearch genre specifically, distinct from this wiki's ES-vs-GRPO weight-space conflict. *(synthesis)*
- **Agent-harness self-modification is emerging as its own subtheme, distinct from weight-space self-improvement.** The watchlist's RRSI (regularized recursive self-improvement of agent harnesses) and DAIR.AI's ClawGym II/JIT-Agent picks apply evolutionary/RL pressure to the scaffold layer, arriving alongside growing alignment scrutiny of what harness-level autonomy implies (assistant-vs-delegate alignment gap, capability laundering). *(synthesis)*
