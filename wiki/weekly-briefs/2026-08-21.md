# Trends in the industry

- **The OPD/teacher-student lineage is converging on "which signal to trust," not new teacher architectures.** GC-OPD (arXiv:2608.19181) calibrates dense teacher-likelihood against a task verifier via a group-normalized residual; ADRS (arXiv:2608.03223) gates self-distilled reward by within-group confidence–return covariance. Both extend, rather than replace, the existing 13-page OPD lineage — the open design question has shifted from *how to teach* to *when the teacher's signal is worth believing*. Directly informs any R_w distillation-signal design in [[research/synthesis/proposed-method]].
- **Process-reward design is shifting toward LLM-judge trace-level rewards over trained PRMs or external verifiers.** CEDAR-GRPO (this week) and ExpRL (last week) both replace step-labeled/trained reward models with a frozen LLM judge scoring something withheld from the policy. Cheap to implement, but both papers carry an acknowledged circularity risk (judge quality and policy quality aren't cleanly separated) — a caveat worth carrying into any R_w reward-design choice that leans on LLM-judge scoring.
- **A second domain now corroborates "competition itself doesn't help" in self-play.** The legal-reasoning negative result (arXiv:2608.01559) reaches the same structural conclusion as its math/code-domain predecessors: value comes from curriculum/environment construction, not the live adversarial mechanism. Three independent domains now point the same direction — this bears directly on whether the project's method needs an adversarial component at all, or just a well-constructed curriculum.
- **"Does privileged self-distillation add genuine capability, or only compact/calibrate what's already there?" is now a live, unresolved question with a new conflict file to show for it.** ADRS's +11.9pp unseen-split transfer sits in soft tension with OPSD's compaction-not-correction claim — see the new [[../conflicts/adrs-vs-opsd-compaction]]. This is the sharpest version yet of a tension that's been building since the 2026-07-31 sweep flagged it informally.
- *(synthesis)* Outside the wiki's direct scope but worth naming: this week's frontier model releases (Qwen3.8-27B, GLM-5.3, DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813) are each attributing gains explicitly to scaled RL post-training on large agentic/environment distributions, not pretraining scale — a macro-trend backdrop that makes the wiki's smaller-scale post-training RL mechanics (GRPO-family, OPD, process rewards) more, not less, load-bearing for understanding where those gains actually come from.

# Top 3 from the watchlist

1. **ADRS: Agentic RL with Self-Distilled Reward Shaping** — folds return-gated, token-level self-distilled reward shaping directly into GRPO/GiGPO's advantage computation (not an auxiliary loss); SOTA on three agentic benchmarks and opens a new capability-vs-compaction conflict against OPSD. *(new, arXiv:2608.03223)*
2. **Does the Competitive Component of Adversarial Self-Play Improve Legal Reasoning?** — controlled negative result: isolating competition from curriculum/data finds no benefit across four tests plus a strengthened-adversary pilot; first self-play theme entry from a non-math/code domain. *(new, arXiv:2608.01559)*
3. **CEDAR-GRPO** — adds LLM-judged evidence-coverage + directionality rewards to GRPO; unrewarded process metrics improve as a side effect while correctness-only GRPO measurably suppresses them, a second independent corroboration of the outcome-only-RL-shortcut pattern. *(new, arXiv:2608.14791)*
