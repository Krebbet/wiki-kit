# Trends in the industry

- **On-policy distillation (OPD) is still the hottest post-training topic.** [[waypoint-opd]] (ingested this week) joins cross-tokenizer OPD, multi-teacher OPD with conflict guarding, and the already-ingested [[ride-opd-residual-extrapolation]] / [[dopd-dual-on-policy-distillation]]; five OPD papers sit in the HF top 15.
- **Harness/scaffold evolution is replacing weight updates as the self-improvement axis.** [[self-evolving-agent-harness]] (ingested), Harness², TreeFlex, ReHarness, Sakana MASS/Agentic Scientist, RSI-Anything, AgentGarten all evolve the scaffold, not the weights. *(synthesis: continues the harness-as-training-target cluster from 2026-09-26.)*
- **Open frontier MoEs are converging on one recipe.** [[reflection-beam]] (501B/23B-active, Apache-2.0), [[muse-spark-open]] (Meta, 816B/42B-active) and Nemotron 3.5 share 256-expert/8-routed MoE routing, 1M context, and day-0 TRL/vLLM/SGLang support; attention designs differ (Beam: sliding-window + sparse global; Muse Spark: GQA with a ~7x larger KV cache). All benchmark claims are vendor-reported.
- **RL infrastructure is the practitioner hotspot.** Async and tree-packed rollouts, bitwise train-inference consistency, memory-lean full-weight RL ([[logra]]), and QwenGyre's rollout/training GPU sharing. Gains are vendor-reported (e.g. 26x trainer-compute cut, 1.9x).
- **Looped/recurrent and delta-rule architectures keep attracting attention, and recurrent-state quantization is a new serving concern.** LoopVL (looped VLM, 484 monthly HF upvotes) and STEPQuant (where quantization error matters in delta-rule state). Builds on [[hyperloop-transformers]].
- **Eval integrity is under pressure.** Benchmark leakage in released RL tasks, harness-dependent scores (ARC-AGI-3), AI-written papers failing integrity checks, and [[co-cheating-crossfit]] ("False Frontiers") at #1 on HF October. *(synthesis: same root as reward-drift in self-evolving loops.)*
