# Trends in the industry

- **The OPD-mechanism-interrogation saga keeps deepening rather than resolving.** *(synthesis)* Six independent papers across five weekly sweeps now bear on [[conflicts/opsa-vs-opd-pattern-transfer]] from six different angles; this week's two additions (UECR-GRPO quantifying naive-teacher/verifier disagreement at 39–51% of pairs, IER adding a third orthogonal reliability axis — gradient-estimation noise, distinct from low-logp suppression and cross-mode capability access) each add evidence without letting either original position win. The pattern itself is becoming the finding: "why does OPD work with so little teacher signal" appears to have several simultaneously-true, non-exclusive answers.
- **Multi-teacher on-policy distillation is emerging as the industry's central open-models debate.** *(synthesis)* Interconnects (Nathan Lambert) frames N-domain-specialist-teachers-into-one-general-student as 2026's most consequential open-models design question, with DeepSeek V4 and Nemotron 3 Ultra reportedly scaling past 10 teachers. No single canonical paper yet — tracked as a trend, not captured — but directly touches this wiki's teacher-student-rl theme and its multi-teacher subtree (MAD-OPD, MT-OPD, One-Student-Many-Teachers).
- **"Reuse, not installation" keeps getting independently reconfirmed by different causal methods and behaviors.** This week's context-grounding paper (arXiv:2609.00925) extends the wiki's core RLVR-mechanics thesis via attention-head knockout — a different method than [[research/rlvr-mechanics/rethinking-rl-sparse-selection]]'s token-logit analysis — and, notably, extends it beyond RLVR to SFT and DPO. Its sharpest result: DPO produces the *largest* behavioral change tested yet shows the same circuit-reuse signature as small-effect GRPO arms — effect size and mechanism-reuse are decoupled.
- **Self-play found a new regime this week: pretraining from zero data, not post-training a capable base model.** Self-Play Pretraining with Zero Data (arXiv:2609.30063) co-evolves a generator and learner from random initialization on synthetic UTM programs alone, with a gradient-alignment reward against the learner's own trajectory — and gets universal cross-modal scaling plus emergent ICL with zero natural data. Distinct enough from the wiki's existing self-play corpus (all RLVR post-training) that it's explicitly *not* in tension with the Invisible Leash family's base-model-support claim — there's no pre-existing base-model mass to reweight here.
- **In-context-learning theory keeps accumulating competing formal accounts rather than converging.** This week's Bayesian-unification paper (arXiv:2609.05111) chains "ICL is Bayes" forward into SFT and KL-regularized RL, but treats it as a *demotion* of the wiki's current lead framing — the "ICL as implicit gradient descent" account is downgraded to a secondary algorithmic realization, not extended. Worth revisiting [[research/in-context-learning-theory/_overview]]'s ordering.
