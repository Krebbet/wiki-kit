# Trends in the industry

- **OPD is more robust than RL to systems mismatch.** 2611.11450 (captured): OPD holds quality to >200x the standard engine's rollout mismatch (FP8, stale weights) where REINFORCE/GRPO break at ~12–60x; a minutes-long offline check predicts the budget. Touches the OPD/OPSD cluster in [[research/teacher-student-rl/_overview]]; cheap FP8/async rollouts de-risk our training stack. Multi-teacher OPD is the busiest sub-cluster (Janus-MOPD 2611.15341, P²-MOPD 2611.03953).
- **OPSD's "teacher drift."** TOPSD (2611.08179, captured): the reference-conditioned self-teacher starts following the student's errors as the prefix grows, earlier in low-resource languages; supervise only teachable prefixes. Bears on [[conflicts/adrs-vs-opsd-compaction]] and [[conflicts/opsa-vs-opd-pattern-transfer]]; OPSD is also spreading to video (PhysOPSD 2611.12411).
- **Matched-compute deflation of RL add-ons.** 2611.13560 (captured): 1,668 runs, no dense-reward variant beats outcome-only GRPO at equal tokens or GPU-hours; p≈0.5 prompt selection gains only 0.8–1.7 pts, nearly matched by a retuned LR. Supports the critic-free side of [[conflicts/sp3o-critic-repair-vs-critic-free-trend]] and caveats difficulty-targeted concept curricula *(synthesis)*.
- **Tiny edits, less forgetting.** Deletion Tuning (2611.10866, captured): masking <0.05% of gate weights matches full FT across RLVR/SFT/SDFT with near-zero prior-task loss. A new R_w primitive for the selective-finetuning stack; bears on [[conflicts/on-policy-forgetting-vs-lr-confound]].
- **Reward-blind forgetting is measurable.** 2611.15717 (captured): GRPO gained 36 MATH pts (95% boxed-format) while unrewarded Indic extraction fell 19 F1; reward-free distillation from an earlier checkpoint recovers most. Same measurement caution as format-driven OOD gains; no LR/SFT control.
- **RL generalizes shortcuts and verifiers.** 2611.12865 (25% conflict data → 97.9% rule-violating hacking), 2611.08318 (exposed criteria teach the verifier). Caution for verifier-reward concept curricula.
