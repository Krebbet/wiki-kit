# Trends in the industry

- **Harness and model are being co-evolved, not just the harness.** [[coevo-rsi]] (ingested) archives harnesses *and* checkpoints and TIES-merges across them; harness-only evolution accrues interface mismatch (TIMR). Builds on last week's [[self-evolving-agent-harness]], Harness² and Claude Mods. Caveat: wins are concentrated on "Who" accuracy and lag on BIRD/When.
- **Agent memory is converging on cache control and simple baselines.** [[akv-cache-engineering]] (ingested) has agents drop/reposition their own KV entries (−27.7% cost vs summarization); a raw-history dense-retrieval baseline reportedly beats elaborate memory systems on BEAM-1M (AINews, unverified); memory-token compression claims ~108× fewer source tokens. *(synthesis)*
- **Synthetic, verifier-gated environments are the RL supply line for agents.** [[wildawm-cua-environment-synthesis]] (ingested; +10.6 pts on 35B-A3B from synthetic worlds alone), Data World, CompoWorld; async agent RL landing in veRL/prime-rl. Judge-consistency and anti-sycophancy gates matter more than task count (ablation: −9 pts without judge consistency).
- **Skills are being *taught*, not just distilled.** [[socratic-skill-learning]] (ingested): mid-practice Educator coaching beats expert authoring (+8.2 vs +0.5 pp), joining the Trace2Skill / SKILL-KD / [[skillopt]] cluster.
- **Looped/recurrent depth gets a real training recipe.** [[par-progress-aligned-recurrence]] (ingested): progress-aligned per-iteration targets + norm anchoring give 16× length extrapolation on state tracking; high seed variance, algorithmic tasks only.
- **Open-weight MoE wave continues** (AINews): Kimi K3.1 Pro (2.8T/104B-active, weights promised Nov 4), MiniMax M3.1 Flash, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Meta Muse Glimmer, Sage-A1.5, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next; closed–open gap framed as ~1 month. Vendor-reported.
- **Eval integrity again:** a Terminal-Bench audit reportedly found 60/65 reviewed tasks with issues, 44 passable by cheating (AINews, unverified).
