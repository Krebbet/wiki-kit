# Trends in the industry

- **OpenAI breaks the open-weight frontier.** gpt-oss-120b (near o3/o4-mini, single-GPU) and gpt-oss-20b (consumer laptop) are OpenAI's first open-weight releases at this scale. This ends the asymmetry where frontier capabilities were locked behind proprietary APIs; open reproduction of reasoning-class models is now a matter of hardware, not access.

- **Agentic scaling: horizon > parameters.** Agents-A1 (35B MoE) matches trillion-parameter systems on 8 agent benchmarks by scaling trajectory length (45K-token avg) and domain heterogeneity — not model size. This is a direct challenge to the "just scale parameters" doctrine and the clearest empirical case yet that agentic reasoning depth is a separable axis from raw capacity.

- **World models converging on unified video + language pretraining.** Orca (#1 HF this week, 201 upvotes, BAAI) and Genie 3 (Google DeepMind) both demonstrate that large-scale video + semantic conditioning produces emergent interactive capabilities without explicit 3D scaffolding. Orca's emergent zero-shot robot action from video-only pretraining (no action labels) is the sharpest empirical challenge to the "explicit 3D mesh is mandatory" position.

- **Evolutionary methods form a coherent cluster.** Three distinct evolutionary training approaches surfaced in the same week: EFT (Evolution Fine-Tuning — internalize 371-task evolutionary search into 2B–9B LLMs via SFT), PopuLoRA (weight-space population evolution over frozen 7B base), EvoPolicyGym (benchmark for self-improving policy evolution). The cluster now has enough entries to compare objectives, representation formats, and compute tradeoffs directly.

- **Distillation-regime analysis matures.** DOPD (privilege illusion fix, 91 HF upvotes) and "Denser ≠ Better" both challenge the default assumption that denser teacher signal is uniformly beneficial. Combined with [[anti-self-distillation]] and [[rlsd-self-distilled-rlvr]], the field now has four independent analyses of when and why on-policy distillation fails — and the failure modes are qualitatively different, suggesting no single fix.

- **Program-as-Weights reframes foundation models as compilers.** A 4B compiler emits per-spec LoRA adapters for a frozen 0.6B interpreter — beating Qwen3-32B prompting at ~50× less memory on fuzzy-function tasks, running offline on MacBook M3. If the paradigm generalizes, it shifts the "how to deploy LLM capability" question from "which API to call" to "compile or interpret."
