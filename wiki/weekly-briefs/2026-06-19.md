# Trends in the industry

- **RL-as-selection cluster is gaining formal grounding.** Chen et al. (2506.04695) provides the first *theoretical* account at the reasoning-pattern level: patterns have stable success rates under RL; RL reshapes which patterns are used, not their competence. Wang et al. (2506.01939, NeurIPS 2025) is the prescriptive counterpart: restricting gradients to the top-20% entropy "fork" tokens matches or improves full-gradient RLVR, with a strong positive scaling trend on Qwen3 14B/32B (+4–11 pp on AIME). The cluster now has empirical (token-reranking), information-geometric (KL asymmetry), and theoretical (pattern-distribution) legs — directly tightening [[conflicts/invisible-leash-vs-spiral-transfer]] Position A. The design implication for the wiki thesis: the training example must lie in the high-entropy region of the base model, not just be "correct."

- **Curriculum RL moving from difficulty-ordering to hint-level adaptivity.** AdaBack (2506.18110) introduces a third curriculum axis — *how much of the correct solution to reveal, per sample, per step* — distinct from task-difficulty ordering (E2H, SCRL) and subproblem decomposition (SCRL). It solves synthetic hard-explore tasks where both SFT and pure RL fail entirely. This is a conceptual shift: the curriculum question is no longer just "what to ask next" but "how much teacher signal to reveal at each attempt." Directly relevant to the wiki's inner-loop design in [[research/synthesis/concept-curriculum-method]].

- **Concept-level training units arriving in post-training.** CAFT (2506.07833) redefines the fine-tuning unit as multi-token concept-spanning sequences. This is the first post-training instantiation of the concept-unit primitive the wiki thesis has been building toward. It is orthogonal to HRM's architectural approach (concept-units in the loss vs. concept-hierarchy in the recurrent module) — both confirm independently that token-granularity is the wrong abstraction for concept learning.

- **Hierarchical architectures achieve extreme sample efficiency without CoT.** HRM (2506.21734, 27M params, 1000 training examples) achieves near-perfect Sudoku and maze reasoning in a single forward pass without pre-training or CoT supervision. Two-timescale recurrence (slow/abstract + fast/detailed) substitutes for data volume. This is an existence proof for the concept-granularity architecture hypothesis and opens the variable-granularity/ theme in the wiki. Friction point for the wiki's current framing: HRM is a pre-training-free scratch model — not post-training on a large backbone — so the "concept installation via fine-tuning" path is not tested here.

- **RLVR theory is converging on the base model quality bottleneck as load-bearing.** Chen et al.'s hard-optimisation regime (RLVR fails when base model reasoning quality is insufficient), the Invisible Leash Stage-1 scope, and BOLT's coverage-ESS frontier ($N \gtrsim 1/p_\gamma$) are all pointing at the same design constraint: the chosen training example must be *latently soluble* by the base model. This is now the single most actionable constraint from the theory side for the proposed method.
