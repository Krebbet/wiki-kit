# Trends in the industry

- **On-policy distillation (OPD) is the hottest post-training topic, now in a refinement phase.** At least 5 OPD papers hit HF this week: [[ride-opd-residual-extrapolation]] (extrapolates the RL teacher's hidden-state residual so the student can pass the teacher), same-family OPD scaling laws (smaller, well-trained teachers often supervise better), UniEvo-VL, and multi-teacher variants. Builds on [[dopd-dual-on-policy-distillation]], [[flux-opd]], [[one-shot-opd-data-efficiency]].
- **SFT is being rehabilitated against RL.** [[sft-with-sampling]] rewrites expert traces via MH projection sampling so plain SFT beats OPSD and RL on chemistry/math with less forgetting (not uniformly: OPSD still wins medical). *(synthesis: pairs with the OPD cluster — both attack the off-policy gap.)*
- **Context management is moving inside the model.** [[context-language-models]] (Meta) lets the LM rewrite its own context as an editable file; Recursive Language Models (watchlisted, 2512.24601) is trending again via a Latent Space interview. Joins [[memagent]] and [[neural-garbage-collection]].
- **Self-evolving loops keep exposing reward-drift failure modes.** [[co-cheating-crossfit]]: proposer and solver agree on shared errors, so internal reward rises without real gains; cross-fitting fixes it (+8–9 pts). Meanwhile Z.ai claims a production outer RSI loop (company-reported, weights withheld).
- **Nested Learning reaches vision.** [[visionhope]] ports HOPE to backbones with a stability fix; competitive on ImageNet/COCO/ADE20K but gains over TTT baselines are ≤0.3pp with no seeds.
- **Looped/recurrent models and consumer-hardware inference are growing clusters.** LoopCD (decode-time contrast of recurrent passes, Ouro-2.6B AIME24 61.9→73.3), Edge0 (35B MoE from SSD at ~20 tok/s), HC-DLM diffusion LMs.
