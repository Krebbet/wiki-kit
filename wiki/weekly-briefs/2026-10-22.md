# Trends in the industry

Quiet week (7-day window). Only two dated vendor items and ~8 arXiv candidates surfaced; most are simulation. Nothing below is padded.

- **Software-side SNN efficiency on commodity silicon is now measured, not just claimed.** SpikeDuet (arXiv:2610.24677) reports 1.27–1.87× speedup and 11–34% lower energy per inference vs sparse-CPU on an EFR32MG24 MCU (measured, inference only; no ANN or neuromorphic baseline). *Evidenced* for MCU-class; it narrows the case that sparsity needs neuromorphic silicon. (synthesis) Touches viability timeline.
- **Analog on-chip learning keeps arriving as simulation.** CwSG (arXiv:2610.27910) trains CNNs with single unverified sub-1 V pulses, replaying measured trajectories of 1,189 PCM / 1,268 filamentary-memristor devices — no array trained, no energy figure. Third simulation-only data point on the open training-viability conflict; the fabricated-array result is still missing.
- **Loihi 2 toolchain reality check.** NeuroML2Loihi (arXiv:2610.25424) is a compiler path to real Loihi 2 hardware (302-neuron C. elegans model), with hand-tuned parameters and a non-like-for-like Jetson energy comparison; it still sits on Lava, which the wiki records as archived. Fault recovery for many-core neuromorphic systems (SpikeRelay) is simulator-only.
- **Money/deployment: BrainChip is broadening to licensable IP and MCU form factors.** Sequora gen-AI IP (2026-10-14, BrainChip, claimed up to 3B-parameter models; no benchmark, power figure or named licensee) and the AKD1500 SPI co-processor chip (2026-10-22; only a third-party market forecast quoted). *Claimed.* BrainChip's September-quarter 4C is not yet lodged.
