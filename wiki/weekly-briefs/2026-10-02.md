# Trends in the industry

- **Multi-teacher OPD now has diagnostic papers, not just recipes.** *(synthesis)* Two papers this week (arXiv:2610.02179, 2609.35347) trace the "student can't beat the best single specialist" failure to Adam shrinking teacher differences and to unequal feedback spread across domains. Extends the teacher-student-rl cluster and the multi-teacher thread from the 09-25 brief.
- **"On-policy ⇒ less forgetting" is being challenged.** arXiv:2609.35259 finds KL direction and learning rate, not rollout policy, drive distillation performance and forgetting. Opens [[conflicts/on-policy-forgetting-vs-lr-confound]] against RL's Razor and the RFT-forgetting pages; settings differ, so not a clean contradiction. Touches [[research/synthesis/recursive-concept-learning]].
- **Sharpening-narrows-RLVR keeps getting reconfirmed from new angles.** Adapter Thickets (2610.00991), Sharpening Tax (2610.01509) and the language-drift proof (2610.02015) all point to RLVR reusing/narrowing rather than installing — reinforces the invisible-leash framing.
- **Random/spurious rewards recast as a reachability probe** (2610.01066): two checkpoints at 3.5% accuracy diverge to 8.5% vs 55% under identical RL. Reframes the spurious-rewards page for single-sample RLVR.
- **Taskless transfer is a new sample-efficiency axis** (ATD, 2609.29233): one teacher-chosen word per prompt on near-indifferent prompts transfers capability between related models — relevant to how little signal a concept update needs.
