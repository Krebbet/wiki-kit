# Trends in the industry

- **The GRPO group-std debate compounded rather than resolved this week — three independent papers converged on the same open conflict.** [[research/rl-optimizers/grpo-std-identity]] (arXiv:2607.00152) proves GRPO/Dr.GRPO/DAPO are three settings of one group-std identity, reframing [[conflicts/mcpo-vs-dr-grpo-std-fix]] as a tradeoff between two valid objectives rather than a bug/fix pair. [[research/rl-optimizers/grpo-length-invariance-impossibility]] (arXiv:2607.23364) adds an independent, orthogonal impossibility result on the *length*-bias axis of the same objective family. [[research/rl-optimizers/cue-grpo-rarity-credit]] (arXiv:2608.03467) sidesteps rather than resolves [[conflicts/mcpo-vs-dapo-mastered-prompts]]. Net effect: the field is actively formalizing *why* no single "done right" GRPO fix exists, which directly informs which normalization choice this wiki's own method should make and why. *(synthesis)*
- **Selective/targeted teacher injection is displacing blanket on-policy distillation.** [[research/teacher-student-rl/rstg-selective-negative-group-distillation]] (RSTG, arXiv:2608.00782) only activates teacher distillation on GRPO's all-fail zero-variance groups (3.63% of data) — naive blanket GRPO+OPD *regresses* math performance. This extends a pattern already on the wiki (EGRSD, ATESD gate teacher signal by student state) and adds a new axis — *which groups* get any signal at all — to the open [[conflicts/knowrl-vs-rlt-hint-design]] hint-design debate.
- **A same-model context-conditioning family is emerging as a lighter-weight alternative to real teacher/student setups.** [[research/rl-optimizers/ice-instruction-conditioned-exploration]] (arXiv:2608.02087) sits alongside [[research/rl-optimizers/sdpg-self-distilled-policy-gradient]] as a second same-parameter, context-conditioned self-distillation method (differing in KL direction) — worth a dedicated comparison note next time either theme gets a full /research pass. It independently reproduces the pass@1-up/pass@256-down signature this wiki already tracks via [[research/self-play/invisible-leash]].
- **Industry-wide, lab blogs were quiet on post-training method content this week; all signal was bottom-up arXiv.** Anthropic, DeepMind, Meta FAIR, Thinking Machines, AI2, and Qualcomm published nothing in-scope this week — consistent with the pattern from the last several sweeps that GRPO-variant churn happens off the big-lab blogs, in independent academic groups (Tianjin/Meituan, Alibaba/Tsinghua, South China UT, Southampton this week alone). *(synthesis)*
