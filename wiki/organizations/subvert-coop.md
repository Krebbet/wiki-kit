# Subvert.fm

Subvert.fm is a cooperatively owned digital music marketplace that launched publicly in July 2026 with 22,742 owner/members — artists, labels, workers, and supporters — as an explicit successor to Bandcamp following its 2022–2023 corporate acquisition and union-busting layoffs. The platform charges 0% platform fees (artists keep 100% of sales minus payment-processor pass-throughs), funds operations through optional fan contributions at checkout, and bans AI-generated music. Governance follows a one-vote-per-member model. Founders have invoked the "Mondragon of Music" framing to describe their ambition for a cooperative music infrastructure federation.

## Structure and governance

Subvert is structured as a cooperative with dual-entity legal architecture. Ownership and governance are held collectively by members; no mandatory platform fee is extracted from sales. At checkout, fans are presented with optional contribution amounts (5%, 10%, 15%, or custom) to fund operations voluntarily. Artist membership is free and requires verification of human creative authorship — AI-generated music is prohibited for sale.

## Origin

Bandcamp was sold to Epic Games in March 2022, then to Songtradr in September 2023. After the second sale, roughly half the staff was laid off, including most of the union bargaining team. Subvert's founders — including Austin Robey, co-founder of music crowdfunding cooperative Ampled and venture-backed Metalabel — responded with a 2024 essay, "A Collectively Owned Bandcamp Successor," proposing a marketplace owned by the people who use it. Thousands signed up within weeks; by October 2025 the co-op had 10,000 members and entered private alpha; by April 2026 membership exceeded 20,000. Public launch followed in July 2026.

## Fee model

| Item | Subvert | Bandcamp |
|---|---|---|
| Platform cut on sales | 0% | 10–15% |
| Listing fee | None | None |
| Fan-side contribution | Optional (5/10/15/custom%) | N/A |
| Waived-fee promotions | N/A | ~6× per year ("Bandcamp Friday") |

## Scale and context

At public launch, Subvert had approximately 22,742 members. Bandcamp, by comparison, hosts hundreds of thousands of artists and generated over $250 million in sales in the prior year. Hypebot (source) recommends a multi-platform approach for artists rather than full migration, citing audience reach differential.
