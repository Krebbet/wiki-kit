# Seattle Fair Pricing and Transparency Act (CB 121267)

Seattle City Council passed CB 121267, which would prohibit using a consumer's personal data to change the price of groceries and other essential items; Consumer Reports calls it the first city-level US ban on personalised pricing. At capture it awaited Mayor Wilson's signature and the bill text had not been captured. Claims below are from a Consumer Reports press release and are attributed accordingly.

## Scope (as reported by CR)

- **Prohibition:** personal data may not be used to alter the price a consumer sees for groceries and essentials. Data categories named: web-browsing history, real-time location, inferences about income, family size, health conditions, and more.
- **Permitted:** a wide range of discounting practices.
- **Added duties:** increased transparency around discounts; some limits on how consumers can be profiled.
- **Status:** passed Council; mayoral signature pending; first-of-kind claim scoped to personalised consumer pricing (SF, Philadelphia and Minneapolis municipal bans concern landlord rent-setting software, a different domain; see [[rental-housing-algorithmic-pricing]]).

## Role of Consumer Reports

CR gave technical assistance to the Mayor's office, testified, and submitted a support letter. Its prior work underpins the record: Kroger loyalty-profile investigation (May 2025), the Instacart same-basket study with Groundwork Collaborative and More Perfect Union (about 400 shoppers, Dec 2025), and an Uber/Lyft investigation. See [[consumer-facing-dynamic-pricing]].

## Framing note

CR's background paragraph says Maryland, Connecticut and New Jersey "ban" surveillance pricing. This is advocacy shorthand; Connecticut pairs a restriction with a prescribed price-increase label (see [[regulatory-responses]], Oct 1 section). The enumerated data categories give a taxonomy a detector could test against, and a city is a lower-barrier venue for local campaigns than a state legislature *(editorial)*; cf. [[tools/natural-price]], [[tools/consumer-price-tools]].
