# noyb v. Scale AI (Outlier): Platform-Worker Surveillance Complaints

In late October 2026 (release updated 29.10.2026; captured 2026-11-02; exact filing date not stated) [[noyb]], with Intérêt à Agir, DiPLab and Reversing.Works, filed five GDPR complaints in France, Italy, the Netherlands and Austria against Scale AI, operator of the Outlier AI-training platform (900,000+ "freelance" workers; customers named include Meta, OpenAI, xAI, Google, Anthropic and the US government). Allegations: consentless third-party trackers, repeated biometric verification via Persona, excess data collection, and unanswered access requests. noyb asks for full investigations and administrative fines.

## Allegations

- **Trackers:** per noyb's technical analysis, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, Sprig and Datadog trackers load while workers are on Outlier and send IPs, contact details, unique identifiers and visited-page data every few seconds. Usable for ad targeting and profiling.
- **Biometrics:** workers must repeatedly pass Persona verification (face video plus ID upload). Scale AI justifies it as fraud prevention and skills verification; noyb says it exceeds necessity. Persona's terms reserve collection of IP, device data, geolocation, addresses and consumer credit data.
- **GDPR grounds:** no valid legal basis for trackers without consent; data minimisation (trackers and biometrics); no response to access requests (Art. 15).
- **Context:** 40M+ EU platform workers per the cited Council infographic; misclassification as self-employed excludes them from social protections. Part of AI-WORLD (working conditions across AI value chains), funded by the European AI & Society Fund and Digital Freedom Fund. DiPLab report: "Surveillance at Scale".
- **Correction:** release amended 29.10.2026 to remove a claim about an October 2025 Persona ID-photo leak. Do not cite that claim.
