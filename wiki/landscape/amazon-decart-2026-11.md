# Amazon buys Decart's optimization stack and team (2026-11-23)

Amazon signed an agreement to buy the technology behind the Decart Optimization Stack (DOS) and bring over Decart's co-founders and research and engineering team, subject to regulatory clearance. **Watch: relevant to Trainium cost-performance claims; if you depend on DOS on non-Amazon hardware, plan for loss of support** *(inference)*.

## Amazon states

- Buying the DOS technology; co-founders Dean Leitersdorf, Moshe Shalev and Orian Leitersdorf and their team join. No purchase of the whole company is described; no price.
- Signed, not closed.
- Work together since 2025 with strong Trainium results (unquantified). No mechanisms or benchmarks disclosed.

## Reported only

- $7.5B (Bloomberg, relayed by TechCrunch 2026-11-23). TechCrunch's wording is unclear on whether the figure covers the technology or the company.
- TechCrunch: DOS runs on Nvidia, Amazon and Google hardware; Amazon is preparing to sell Trainium to customers. Future DOS availability on other hardware is not addressed.

## History

Anthropic was reported in talks to buy Decart at about $6B (2026-08), later reported withdrawn. See watchlist entries of 2026-08 and 2026-10-28; both unconfirmed.
