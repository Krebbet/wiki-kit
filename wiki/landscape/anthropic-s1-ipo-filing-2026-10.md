# Anthropic S-1 IPO filing (2026-10-26)

A secondary aggregator (Let's Data Science, captured 2026-11-04) reports Anthropic, PBC filed a Form S-1 on 2026-10-26 for a Nasdaq Global Select listing of Class A stock under "ANT". **Watch:** the filing is a vendor-risk and capital-structure event, not a product one. Every figure below is secondary and unverified; confirm against the primary S-1 on SEC EDGAR before relying on any of it. Share count and price range are blank, and the source reports no valuation, revenue or loss figures.

## Reported facts (secondary, unverified)

- Sellers: the company plus selling stockholders (no proceeds to the company from the latter). 22 underwriters, Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley listed first; over-allotment option. Prospectus "Subject To Completion".
- Share classes:
  - **Class T** — elected and removed by the Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT); Class T directors will be a majority of the authorized board; no other votes, no economic rights.
  - **Class F** — a single share held by a Founder LLC (Dario and Daniela Amodei plus five other co-founders); 50.1% of voting power on matters put to stockholders generally, including non-Class-T directors; no economic rights.
  - **Class E** — non-co-founder employees; limited votes, no economic rights.
  - **Class A** — 1 vote/share. **Class N** — non-voting, held mainly by strategic business partners, generally converts to A on sale to an unaffiliated third party.
- "Controlled company" under Nasdaq rules. Stated public benefit purpose: "the responsible development and maintenance of advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity."
- The aggregator's own analysis (not a company statement): public Class A holders would have limited governance influence.

## Gaps and inconsistencies

- The source does not reconcile Class F's 50.1% with Class T's board-majority election.
- It gives no trustee count, so the earlier "shrunk 3-trustee LTBT" note in [[landscape/anthropic-series-h-2026-05]] can be neither confirmed nor refuted. Not a conflict.
- The earlier "$2T+ IPO target, possibly late Sept 2026" note is unverifiable here; the filing date (2026-10-26) is later than that target, so the timeline slipped.
- Article date is not stated; MD&A and risk-factor content is not summarised.
- Other reports seen during this week's scan disagree on profit and loss figures; none is verified.

No direct signal. Indirect: control of a key model supplier sits with founders and the LTBT, which is a vendor-governance factor for buyers concentrating on Claude.
