# Salesforce Acquires Fin for $3.6B (June 2026)

Salesforce agreed on 2026-06-15 to acquire Fin (formerly Intercom) for $3.6B, folding a production-grade autonomous customer-service agent directly into Agentforce. The deal closes in Salesforce FY2027 Q4 (early calendar 2027). Fin co-founder Eoghan McCabe stays as CEO; co-founder Des Traynor continues leading R&D. The acquisition positions Agentforce against Zendesk and ServiceNow in the autonomous support market, and arrives the same week Salesforce shipped multi-agent orchestration GA in Summer '26.

## What Fin Is

Fin is an AI agent that resolves customer queries end-to-end across live chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, phone, and Slack — omnichannel, not just a chatbot. The company also shipped its own foundation model, Apex, and an internal autonomous agent, Operator. These proprietary assets are a material part of the acquisition rationale alongside the team.

## Strategic Rationale

Agentforce as of mid-2026 lets enterprises build and orchestrate custom agents but has limited out-of-the-box "solved" vertical products. Fin supplies a ready-to-deploy service-agent product with existing enterprise customers and a proof point on resolution rates. Salesforce's stated goal: accelerate time-to-value versus requiring customers to build from scratch.

The competitive read: this is a direct counter to Zendesk's AI ambitions and ServiceNow's Customer Service Management agent layer. Bundling a battle-tested service agent into the Salesforce platform (with Agentforce orchestration, Data Cloud context, and CRM data) raises the switching cost for Salesforce's existing customer base.

## What Changes (Per Fin)

McCabe's public statement signals operational continuity: leadership stays, roadmap accelerates with Salesforce resources. Fin's existing customers are told "little will practically change." The Apex model and Operator agent development continues.

## Context: Salesforce June 2026 Activity

The acquisition lands in a dense week for Salesforce:
- Summer '26 release: multi-agent orchestration GA (same week)
- $1B Italy investment commitment; Milan AI office opened
- Federated search partnership with Databricks announced at Data + AI Summit
