# AI Assistant Market Share Shift — ChatGPT Below 50% (Sensor Tower, June 2026)

ChatGPT fell below 50% AI chatbot market share for the first time by May 2026, despite reaching 1.1B monthly users — a paradox explained by the broader market growing faster than any single player. Gemini surged from 5.4% to 27.7% share in six months on Android/Workspace defaults. Claude holds 10.3% share but leads all assistants on subscription conversion (13% of users pay).

## User numbers (May 2026)

Source: Sensor Tower State of AI Report 2026.

| Assistant | Monthly users | Market share |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| ChatGPT | 1.1B | 46.4% |
| Gemini | 662M | 27.7% |
| Claude | 245M | 10.3% |
| Grok / Perplexity / DeepSeek / Meta AI | — | <5% each |

ChatGPT hit 900M weekly active users (February 2026) and 1.1B MAU by June 2026 — the fastest app ever to reach 1B monthly users. Twelve months prior it commanded ~87% of AI chatbot web traffic; by May 2026 that had fallen to 46.4%.

## Gemini's rise

Gemini's 5.4% → 27.7% share jump in six months is driven primarily by Android default assistant replacement and Google Workspace integration — distribution mechanics, not model quality differentiation. See [[landscape/google-cloud-next-2026-day2]] for the enterprise-side Gemini agent push.

## Claude monetization signal

13% of Anthropic users pay for a subscription as of mid-2026 — the highest conversion rate in the field. Claude's user-retention rate is also closing the gap with ChatGPT. See [[landscape/anthropic-subscription-split-2026-06]] for Anthropic's subscription tier restructure context and [[llms/anthropic-claude-family]] for model family.

## Brand trust as switching trigger

DoD contract announcement (February 2026) triggered a 295% surge in ChatGPT uninstalls. Users demonstrably switch on values/brand events, not only on feature deltas — a signal relevant to enterprise procurement risk in [[landscape/llm-api-enterprise-share]].

## Monetization and engagement

- H1 2026: ~2.3B AI app downloads; $4.2B in-app spending (vs $1.83B H1 2025, +130%)
- Growth rate decelerating despite absolute climb — market-maturation signal
- ChatGPT ran ads for ~17% of daily users by May 2026
- Average revenue per user rising industry-wide
- Time spent: 17.2B hours H1 2025 → ~36B hours H1 2026 (+109%); top 3 assistants command 89% of time spent

## Regional patterns

- Asia: first download decline of 3.3% in Q1 2026 (China + India dip)
- North America + Europe lead in-app spending despite Asia leading total download volume
- Spending growth concentrated in subscription tiers, not one-off purchases

## Shopping / referral

Amazon blocked ChatGPT web crawlers, resulting in stagnant AI-referral traffic to Amazon. Walmart's "Spark" assistant gaining ground vs Amazon's "Rufus" in the shopping-assistant vertical.
