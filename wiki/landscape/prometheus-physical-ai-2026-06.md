# Prometheus — Artificial General Engineer ($12B, 2026-06)

Prometheus is a physical-AI startup co-founded by Jeff Bezos and Vik Bajaj (co-CEO) that is building what it calls an "artificial general engineer" — software to automate design and manufacturing of complex physical systems. Founded November 2025, it has raised $18.2B in two rounds at a $41B post-money valuation, making it one of the most richly capitalized AI startups ever. As of June 2026, it has 150 employees and no public product; the bulk of capital is directed at compute.

## What it is

Prometheus is building AI tools for engineers, not robots. Bezos has been explicit: "Nothing to do with robotics." The stated goal is to automate the design of complex physical objects — jet engines, drug compounds, semiconductor architectures — by training models on the physical and mathematical laws governing how those objects behave and fail. The term "artificial general engineer" (AGE) is the company's own framing for a system that generalizes across engineering domains the way a large language model generalizes across language tasks.

The key distinction from software-AI agents (e.g., OpenAI Operator, AWS Bedrock Agents): the target domain is the physical world's design space, not business-process automation or knowledge retrieval. The moat argument is that physical-world data — CAD models, simulation outputs, materials science results — is harder to replicate than text corpora, and physical systems impose hard ground-truth validation that code cannot fake.

## Founders and thesis

**Jeff Bezos** — co-founder and co-CEO. Executive chairman of Amazon (1.5M+ employees). Returning to an operating CEO role for the first time since stepping down at Amazon in 2021. Has framed the startup as a personal bet, not an Amazon adjacency.

**Vik Bajaj** — co-founder and co-CEO. PhD in physical chemistry from MIT; postdoc at UC Berkeley. Co-founded Google Life Sciences in March 2013 (rebranded Verily, 2015), served as founding Chief Scientific Officer until October 2016. Was CSO at Grail (2016–17), then managing director at Foresite Capital and CEO of Foresite Labs until February 2026, when he stepped down to focus on Prometheus. Background straddles life sciences and deep tech — not a pure software AI profile.

**Thesis on labor:** Bezos's public framing inverts the standard displacement narrative. He uses the term **"labor scarcity"** to describe a productivity-rich economy where demand for workers exceeds supply — predicting that AI productivity gains will raise living standards and reduce the need for multi-earner households, rather than creating mass unemployment. This is a minority view among major AI founders.

## Physical AI as a category (2026 framing)

"Physical AI" is the 2026 shorthand for applying foundation-model-scale intelligence to the physical world: robotics, industrial automation, hardware simulation, and engineered-system design. Investors argue the category is inherently more defensible than pure software because physical-world data creates moats that code alone cannot replicate.

Prometheus is the largest single bet, but not isolated:

| Company | Focus | Notable raise |
|---------|-------|--------------|

Q1 2026 saw $6.4B+ raised across 27 physical AI companies, with $4B absorbed by robotics alone (Foundevo tracker). Bessemer Venture Partners published a "50 startups transforming industries with physical AI" list in 2026, signaling category normalization. The week of June 15, 2026 saw three companies using the "Physics AI" label raise a combined $15.8B (StartupHub.ai).

Eclipse's thesis (Apr 2026): "This is the first time where stuff is going to move from our screens into the physical world" — frames physical AI as the next compute-to-application transition after cloud.

## Conflict flags

- **C8 (AGI for enterprise / software agents):** The wiki's coverage of enterprise AI agents (OpenAI Operator, AWS Bedrock, Anthropic Claude in enterprise) is about software-process automation. Prometheus is explicitly *not* a software-agent play — it targets the physical design space. These are distinct markets with different data assets, customers, and validation mechanisms. No direct conflict, but the "AGI" framing overlaps terminologically with software-AI claims and should not be conflated.
- **"Labor scarcity" vs. displacement consensus:** Bezos's framing runs counter to most enterprise AI vendors' messaging (productivity augmentation) and to the displacement-risk consensus at Anthropic/OpenAI. Flag for the wiki's jobs/labor section if one exists.
- **Valuation at $41B pre-product:** Comparable only to OpenAI and Anthropic at similar stages. Implies investors are pricing a winner-take-most outcome in physical-AI design tooling. If the category fragments (domain-specific simulators per vertical), the AGE generalist thesis weakens.
