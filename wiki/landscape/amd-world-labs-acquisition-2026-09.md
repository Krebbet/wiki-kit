# AMD to acquire World Labs for $8.2B in stock — 2026-09

CNBC (article dated 2026-09-28; captured 2026-09-30) reports AMD agreed to acquire Fei-Fei Li's world-model startup World Labs for $8.2B, all stock, with Li becoming AMD chief scientist and EVP. Close expected later in 2026; World Labs stays separate from AMD's chipmaking until then. AMD was a prior investor. It is AMD's second-largest acquisition after Xilinx (~$50B, 2022). Single-source (CNBC); the strategic rationale is CNBC's framing, not evidenced.

## Facts (2026-09-28)
- World Labs (San Francisco) builds world models that simulate 3D environments; product shown: **Marble**, which generated a 3D scene from a few images in a demo with Li and Lisa Su earlier in 2026. No architecture, revenue, customer or availability data in the source.
- Li: agents (robots, vehicles, tools) can "learn inside very rich physics-aware digital worlds" before deployment, "making them much safer."
- Stated rationale: world-model research informs what AMD chips need "years in advance."

## Context (CNBC's M&A-wave framing)
OpenAI/io (~$6.4B, 2025); Nvidia ~$33B across Groq (assets, December) and Hugging Face (agreed September 2026, "almost $13 billion") — the $33B is a CNBC sum, and Groq was an asset deal; Meta's $14B minority stake in Scale AI (2025).
